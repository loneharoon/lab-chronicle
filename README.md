# SIRL Lab Chronicle

This repository maintains the collective chronicle of the Sensing and Informatics Research Lab (SIRL). The chronicle documents the academic and research journey of the lab, including:

- conference experiences,
- lab events,
- student reflections,
- workshops and seminars,
- research milestones,
- and other memorable moments.

The goal is to preserve not only publications and outcomes, but also the learning, collaboration, challenges, and experiences that shape the lab over time.

---

## Sections

- **Conferences**  
  Conference reports, technical takeaways, travel experiences, and reflections.

- **Lab Events**  
  Internal meetings, workshops, celebrations, outreach activities, and special events.

- **Reflections**  
  Personal reflections from students and researchers about research, conferences, failures, growth, and learning experiences.
