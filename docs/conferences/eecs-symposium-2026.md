# EECS Research Symposium 2026
**Date:** March 11-12, 2026  
**Location:** IISER Bhopal, India 

## Overview
All members of SIRL participated in the EECS Research Symposium held at IISER Bhopal, where they showcased their research through oral and poster presentations. The symposium provided an opportunity to present their work to fellow students from the EECS department, invited guests, and faculty members. SIRL maintained a strong presence at the event, with every member contributing through research talks and poster presentations. Notably, Aditya Mishra was recognized with the Best Oral Presenter Award for his presentation.
