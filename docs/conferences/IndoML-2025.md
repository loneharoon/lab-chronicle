# Indian Symposium on Machine Learning 2025
**Date:** December 19-21, 2025  
**Location:** BITS Hyderabad 

## Overview
Aditya Mishra (BSMS 2021–2026) and Vikas (PhD 2025–2030) were selected to participate in IndoML 2025, with their travel and accommodation fully supported by the IndoML organizing committee. During the event, they interacted with professors, researchers, and students from various institutes, and showcased both their individual research contributions and the research activities being carried out at SIRL.
