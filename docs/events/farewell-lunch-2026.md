# Farewell Lunch 2026
**Date:** April 21, 2025  
**Location:** Vantra, Bhopal, India 

## Overview
SIRL organized a farewell lunch to celebrate and honor the graduating batch of 2026. The gathering provided an opportunity for lab members to reflect on shared experiences, research achievements, and memorable moments. PI and students came together to extend their best wishes to the graduating students as they embarked on the next stage of their academic and professional journeys.
