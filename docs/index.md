# Lab Chronicle

Welcome to the Lab Chronicle.

This website documents the academic, technical, and collaborative journey of the lab, including conference experiences, research reflections, workshops, lab events, and other important milestones.

The chronicle serves as a shared archive of ideas, experiences, and memories contributed by lab members over time.

## Why a Chronicle?

Research is more than publications and deadlines. Conferences, discussions, failed experiments, collaborations, and reflections all contribute to the growth of a research community.

This chronicle aims to preserve those experiences and make them accessible to current and future lab members.
